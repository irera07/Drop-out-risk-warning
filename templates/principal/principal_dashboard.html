How to use EduGuard as a Principal
1

View a class report

Go to Current Report, select a class from the dropdown, and click Check updated report. This displays the latest ML prediction results for that class — generated by the class teacher.

2

View the school-wide summary

Scroll below the class selector to see the school-wide aggregated report — total students, overall dropout risk, and per-class breakdown cards showing high / medium / low risk counts.

3

Identify high-risk classes

The class breakdown cards are clickable — click any class card to jump straight to its detailed prediction table with per-student results.

4

Download reports

Download a single class's results as CSV, or click Download full school report to get a merged CSV of all classes in your school.

5

Browse historical reports

Go to Previous Reports to see all past predictions for your school. Use the class filter to narrow by class, and download any individual report as CSV.
