How to use EduGuard as a Local Leader
1

View a school report

Go to Current Report, select a school from the dropdown, and click Check updated report. This shows the aggregated results across all classes in that school — total students, risk counts by level, and a class-by-class breakdown table.

2

View the sector-wide summary

Scroll below the school selector on the Current Report tab to see the full sector overview — totals across every school in your sector, plus clickable school cards showing per-school risk levels at a glance.

3

Identify high-risk schools

The school summary cards are clickable — click any card to jump straight to that school's detailed class breakdown. Schools with a high proportion of at-risk students will show prominent High risk counts in red.

4

Download reports

Download a single class report as CSV from the class breakdown table, or click Download full sector report to get a merged CSV of all classes across all schools in your sector, with school and class name columns included.

5

Browse historical reports

Go to Previous Reports to see all past predictions across your sector. Use the school filter to narrow results and download any individual report as CSV.
